🔄 APRS Digipeater (New-N Paradigm)

When Digipeater is ON, the application acts as an autonomous digital repeater for other stations. It receives packets on 144.800 MHz, rewrites the path (e.g. WIDE1-1 → MYCALL*), and re-transmits them over 2m via the radio USB audio and CI-V PTT with loop and duplicate suppression.

ms (recommended 200 - 300 ms)
⛅ APRS Weather Station (Live WX)

Turn your station into an autonomous APRS Weather Station. It uses your configured QTH coordinates to fetch real-time local weather (temperature, humidity, barometric pressure, wind) from Open-Meteo (100% free, no API key needed), switches your symbol to /_ Weather Station, and transmits official APRS weather telemetry packets at your beacon interval.

🔒 Frequency Safety Lock

Prevents accidental transmissions outside the APRS frequency or mode. If your radio's VFO knob is turned away from 144.800 MHz (tolerance ±500 Hz) or mode is changed away from FM, all transmissions (beacons and digipeater packets) are strictly blocked to protect other frequencies and voice channels.
